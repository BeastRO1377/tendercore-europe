DATA QUALITY & METHODOLOGY

How it works

Transparent inputs, limited conclusions and traceable sources.

01 · TED Search API

Published competition and award notices from TED v3. A scan retrieves a limited, recent sample. Errors are shown and previous data are kept.

02 · eForms BT-751

Bid guarantee is parsed from XML separately for each lot. Missing values remain unknown. Performance guarantees and fees require document review.

03 · Bid / No-Bid

Your profile filters budgets, CPV, geography and deadlines. Green means preliminary fit, never legal eligibility or guaranteed admission.

04 · Historical evidence

Award notices may show winners, amounts and submissions, subject to completeness. The system never invents win probabilities from budgets.

05 · EU taxes v0.4

All 27 standard VAT rates are available. The financial model uses user-specified treatment and does not automatically decide national tax liability.

06 · Deployment

Local single-user application. Bind to 127.0.0.1; production hosting requires authentication, HTTPS, rate limiting and security review.
